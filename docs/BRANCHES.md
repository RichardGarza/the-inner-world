## Lines written for the game

The book's text is used as written. These are the only passages that are not in the manuscript. Each exists because a branch needed a beat the book didn't have.

- **Fork 2, feeling branch.** Lena: "You don't. You stand there and it chooses you. Then you carry it home." She looked at me one beat too long. "I had you figured for one of the good ones."
- **Fork 3, feeling branch.** "I didn't ask the hard one ... It sat in my throat the rest of the hour, and I'd swear Lena could see it there."
- **Fork 4, concourse branch.** "I took the concourse. Eleven steps in, a screen told me to open my heart and a stranger's shoulder told me to move, and I turned around and went back down to the stairwell."
- **Fork 4, lead-in.** "Two ways home from Nine. The concourse is faster, but the concourse is full of screens, and the screens are full of people who are certain." (Rearranged from the book's own sentence.)
- **Fork 5, lead-in and running branch.** "I crouched at the edge of it. The recorder was still glowing in my pocket." and "I left it running. Upstairs the transcript would read: drip. breath. drip."
- **Fork 6, feeling branch.** "I opened my mouth to tell her about the water. What came out was: 'I ate.'"
- **Fork 7, walking branch.** "I kept walking. I made it as far as the stairs. Then I was standing there again ..."
- **Fork 8, nod branch.** "I nodded. She caught the look on my face anyway, before I'd decided to have one."
- **Fork 9, photograph branch.** "I had the slab halfway out of my pocket before I remembered what it was ... I put it back."
- **The sheet.** The "shape of the day" line and the counted-figures line are generated from play. The three lines above them are the book's.
- **The end card.** "You counted N figures. The city felt N things through you." followed by the book's own "Two things that cannot both be everything" line.

To replace any of these, search `index.html` for the beat's `id` (for example `lq2`, `hard2`, `conc0`, `rec2`, `m2`, `w2`, `cq2`, `ph1`).
